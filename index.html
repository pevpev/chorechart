Questions parents ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The online tool is free, with no account, no ads, and no watermark on what you print.

Do my kids need points?

No. Untick "Use a points system" and you get a simple tick-box chart with no points, allowance, or rewards.

Can I make charts for more than one child?

The free tool makes one chart at a time. Pro lets you keep up to six children and print all their charts at once.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The page adapts to smaller screens. Printing works best from a laptop or desktop.

Can I turn points into allowance money?

Yes. Set a dollar amount per point and the chart shows what a fully completed week is worth. Pro adds a per-child rate and a four-week estimate.
